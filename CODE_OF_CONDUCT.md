# Code of conduct

Iron Untangled is a small fan project for readers. Please help keep it a friendly place.

## Be kind

- Be respectful and assume good faith. People read at different speeds and remember different details; a correction is help, not an attack.
- Critique the map, not the person. No harassment, insults, or discriminatory or sexual content.
- Keep discussion on the book and the project.

## Spoiler etiquette

Issues, pull requests and discussions here talk about the book chapter by chapter, so:

- **Start every title with the chapter**, like `[Ch 12] Wrong station number`.
- **Keep plot details out of titles.** Put them in the body, where people only see them if they open it.
- Don't bring in spoilers from later books or other media, even in the body, unless the issue is about them and says so in the title.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit titles that give things away, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block people who keep breaking these rules. To raise a concern privately, message the maintainer, Ricardo Canales, on Threads ([@canalesb93](https://www.threads.com/@canalesb93)).

Iron Untangled

A spoiler-safe, chapter-by-chapter map and reading companion for Dungeon Crawler Carl: The Dungeon Anarchist’s Cookbook.

Tell it the last chapter you finished and it draws the Iron Tangle as far as the book has revealed it, with a short recap of each chapter. Everything ahead of you stays under fog.

The map needs JavaScript. It is an unofficial fan project and open source on GitHub. The map is a fan interpretation of the book, not an official layout, and most details are unverified.
